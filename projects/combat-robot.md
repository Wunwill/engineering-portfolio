# 12 lb Combat Robot

**Context:** Aggie Robotics, team member, February 2026-present.

## Design Work

- Designed chassis and motor mounts in Onshape using parametric sketches and assemblies.
- Produced dimensioned drawings for waterjet-cut 6061 aluminum side plates.
- Identified motor-to-frame interference through assembly clearance checks.
- Revised mounting-hole locations and added tool access so motors could be replaced without removing the top plate.
- Pocketed chassis plates and revised brackets to reduce assembled weight from 13.1 to 11.8 lb.
- Checked mounting alignment and fastener retention during three drive-test sessions.

## Design Tradeoffs

The design had to address mass, component clearance, manufacturability, and service access together. Removing material helped meet the mass target, while revised mounting geometry and tool access addressed assembly and maintenance constraints.

The assembled weight reduction is 1.3 lb, approximately 9.9% of the starting weight. The final 11.8 lb leaves 0.2 lb below the nominal 12 lb target.

## Skills Demonstrated

Parametric CAD, assembly-fit checks, dimensioned drawings, iteration around physical constraints, and practical mechanical testing.
