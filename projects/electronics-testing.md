# Electronics Testing and Repair

**Context:** CERN A&M Chapter, May-August 2026.

## My Contribution

- Diagnosed and repaired 12 shorted circuits using thermal imaging, microscope inspection, and resistance measurements.
- Verified repairs through continuity checks and powered voltage measurements.
- Reprogrammed 30 power modules for KRIA compatibility and recorded bench-test results.

## Engineering Approach

The work combined fault localization with verification. Thermal imaging helped locate suspect areas, microscope inspection supported component-level examination, and electrical measurements helped investigate the faults. After repairs, continuity checks and powered voltage measurements provided separate checks of the result.

The important distinction is between finding a suspicious component and establishing that the repaired board behaves as expected. Both diagnosis and post-repair checks were part of my work.

## Skills Demonstrated

Systematic troubleshooting, careful handling of sensitive electronics, measurement interpretation, repair verification, and test-result recording.

My programming work involved existing power modules and compatibility changes; the experience described here is bench testing and repair rather than original FPGA or module design.
